2. `python3 .agents/skills/tlc-spec-lean/scripts/check_proofs.py fase4/<feature> --mutations .specs/features/fase4/<feature>/mutations.json` (o OS usa `.specs/features/fase4/servico-os/proof-env/run_proofs.sh`).
